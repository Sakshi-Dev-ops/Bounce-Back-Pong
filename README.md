## 💡 Inspiration & Theme Connection: "Bounce Back"
In **Bounce Back Pong**, every incoming shot is a test of resilience and momentum. As your rally continues, the ball accelerates, forcing you to adapt quickly and *bounce back* under increasing pressure. Whether you are defending your goal in a solo endurance run or staging a comeback against a friend, keeping the rally alive is the key to victory.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Welcome & Theme Showcase:** Introduces the creators and explains how the mechanics tie into the *Bounce Back* theme.
* **Clear Rules & Interactive Tutorial Demo:** Players can jump straight into a match or test their paddle controls in a live, playable mini-arena before starting.
* **Flexible Controls:** Choose between **Mouse** or **Keyboard (`W`/`S`)** in Solo Mode, or play local **2-Player Versus** on one keyboard.
* **Dynamic Speed Scaling:**
  * **Solo Mode:** Earn +10 points per rebound. Every **30 points**, the ball gets faster. Reach **100 points** to win!
  * **2-Player Mode:** Every **2 points** a player scores, the ball speeds up. First to **10 points** wins!
* **Quick Replayability:** Instant restart flow takes players straight back to mode selection for fast rematches.

## 🕹️ How to Play & Controls

| Mode | Player 1 (Left Paddle) | Player 2 (Right Paddle) | Win Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 Player (Solo)** | **Mouse** (Move Up/Down) *or* **`W` / `S`** | *Backboard Wall* | Reach **100 points** |
| **2 Players (Versus)** | **`W`** (Up) / **`S`** (Down) | **`Up Arrow`** / **`Down Arrow`** | First to **10 points** |
